# Sources — THE CALL 002 · Trapped in the Attic (Sumas, Washington, December 2025)

## The Sumas attic rescue
- USCG Northwest District press release (Dec. 12, 2025): 4 people in a flooded home in Sumas, rising water forced them into the attic; Whatcom County Fire District 14 and U.S. Border Patrol requested assistance, could not reach them due to 8–10 ft deep water around the home; Air Stations Port Angeles and Astoria; Port Angeles MH-65 arrived first, swimmer deployed to porch roof, helped survivors through a second-story attic window; Port Angeles hoisted first two to a landing zone while swimmer stayed on roof with the other two; Astoria MH-60 arrived immediately after, hoisted the remaining two and recovered the swimmer; EMS at landing zone; no injuries; Scott Giard quote ("This is exactly why we train the way we do, day in and day out."). Release says "Wednesday" — DVIDS lists Dec. 11; the script therefore gives no specific day for the hoist. https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/4358174/video-coast-guard-rescues-four-people-from-flooded-home-in-sumas-wash-during-hi/
- Fact-check notes: Stevens Pass washouts were on US-2 between Skykomish and Leavenworth; Duvall '4 feet in 4 hours' is one farmer's account to AP; FD14 said Coast Guard came 'in boats'.
- Same facts (Port Angeles + Astoria, attic, up to 10 ft, swimmer, no injuries): https://dailyastorian.com/2025/12/12/astoria-coast-guard-helicopter-rescues-flood-victims-in-washington/ ; https://www.stripes.com/branches/coast_guard/2025-12-12/coast-guard-rescue-washington-flooding-20069176.html ; https://whatcom-news.com/helicopter-rescues-4-from-rooftop-in-sumas-video_233371/

## Weather and rivers
- Atmospheric river Dec 8–11, warm, very high snow levels (much mountain precipitation fell as rain); first week of December moderate mountain rain saturated soils; lowlands ~3–6", windward Cascade/Olympic slopes 10–20"; Lynn Lake 21.80" (highest); Skagit, Nooksack, Snohomish new streamflow records; USGS: 10 rivers set new all-time record crests; Yakima 72°F on Dec 10; 382 daily high-minimum temperature records (Dec 5–19); US-2 washouts cut off Stevens Pass for weeks; Dec 15–18 storm: snow levels dropped (limited flood risk), wind outages to hundreds of thousands of customers statewide. https://climate.uw.edu/?p=10039 (Office of the Washington State Climatologist, December Notable Weather Events)
- Nooksack set a record of just over 150' early Thursday Dec 11 at North Cedarville; Wed night (Dec 10) more than 20 water rescues in Everson, Nooksack, Sumas; Thursday night more than 40 rescue calls to Search and Rescue; Sumas feed mill (region's only) flooded up to 6 ft. https://www.whatcomcounty.us/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=5274
- NWS flood warning 12:40 p.m. Wed Dec 10: Nooksack overflow at Everson "imminent or occurring" along an abandoned side channel, including Johnson Creek, running from Everson north toward Sumas and the Canadian border. https://www.lyndentribune.com/news/flood-warning-through-4-a-m-thursday-dec-11/article_670a1f9b-94e8-4985-aa08-41ca1853f5f9.html
- When the Nooksack overflows, floodwater enters the Sumas River and crosses the border into Abbotsford, B.C.; Nov 2021: Nooksack floodwater added to rain and the Sumas Prairie went underwater; Sumas pop. just over 1,600; 85% of homes and businesses damaged. https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7028268
- Sumas advisory Tue Dec 9 ~3 p.m.; Everson alert ~3 p.m. Wed when river overtopped its banks; Sumas Go Alert ~3:30 p.m. Wed: "Flooding in Sumas is imminent." "It is recommended to evacuate." Leave ASAP because evacuation routes will close once water reaches them. https://whatcom-news.com/sumas-everson-and-nooksack-prepare-their-citizens-for-a-significant-flooding-event_233316/

## Sumas on the ground (Fire District 14 / Bellingham Herald)
- Chief Jerry DeBruin: ~50 people rescued in Sumas on Thursday Dec 11, mostly high-water vehicles; USCG boat crew helped people on the west side of the railroad tracks; a family taken by boat from second floor; woman out on a tractor from a local resident; evacuation order Wednesday afternoon, most residents left; better prepared than Nov 2021 ("We had never seen a flood to that magnitude"); District 14 station flooded ~2.5 ft, command base ~1 mile south; woman back at Columbia Street home trapped by ~6 ft water, rescued by Sumas police officer trained in swim rescue, checked for cold exposure; evacuees taken to Lynden on school buses, road closures made it difficult; few who stayed needed help. https://www.firefighternation.com/?p=182067
- Sumas floodwaters as high as 15 ft; Skagit/Snohomish records; ~78,000 ordered to evacuate Skagit Valley; more than 100,000 statewide told to leave; I-90 landslide east of Seattle; Amtrak suspended Pacific Northwest service; flooding reached Idaho and British Columbia. https://www.heraldnet.com/?p=859469 (The New York Times, republished)
- Skagit crested ~37 ft at Mount Vernon overnight Thu–Fri, a few inches above previous record; flood wall held; ~1,000 Burlington residents evacuated in the middle of the night; two homes near Deming collapsed into the Nooksack after erosion undercut them, no one inside; Sumas border crossing closed; Duvall water rose about 4 ft in 4 hours; President signed the state's emergency declaration request. https://www.clickorlando.com/news/national/2025/12/13/historic-rains-and-flooding-trigger-dramatic-rescues-in-washington-state/ (Associated Press)

## The hunters (Suiattle River Valley)
- Friday (Dec 12, 2025): Astoria aircrew assisting local responders searching the Suiattle Valley spotted two hunters flashing lights at a remote cabin near Mansford; floodwaters washed out their only road access; running out of food; swimmer lowered; hoisted in rescue basket; flown to Darrington Municipal Airport, reunited with families. https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/4359380/video-coast-guard-rescues-2-stranded-hunters-during-historic-flooding-in-wester/ ; date Friday Dec 12: https://coastguardfoundation.org/news/coast-guard-saves-lives-during-widespread-washington-flooding ; footage DVIDS 990071 (MH-60 Jayhawk, Air Station Astoria, PO3 Annika Hirschler) https://www.dvidshub.net/video/990071
