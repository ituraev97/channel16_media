TITLE: Trapped in the Attic as the River Rose: Coast Guard Rooftop Rescue in Sumas, Washington

December 2025. A warm atmospheric river sends record floods across western Washington, and the Nooksack River spills north into the border town of Sumas. Four people are trapped in their attic, with 8 to 10 feet of water around the house and no way for ground crews to reach them. Using real U.S. Coast Guard footage, we follow the call from the river to the roof: a Dolphin crew from Port Angeles puts a rescue swimmer on the porch roof, and a Jayhawk crew from Astoria, Oregon, finishes the job.
