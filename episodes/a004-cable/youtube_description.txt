TITLE: On the Cable: How a Coast Guard Helicopter Hoist Works, in 6 Real Rescues

How do you lift a family off Alaska's sea ice, four mariners off a sinking tug, or an injured hiker out of a mountain gully, from a helicopter? Using real U.S. Coast Guard hoist footage, we break the helicopter hoist down step by step: who runs the cable, why the basket has to touch the deck first, what the trail line is for, and the calls the crew makes from the cabin door. Six real rescues: the tug Sea Ranger in the Gulf of Alaska, the sailboat Walrus off North Carolina, a cruise ship medevac off Washington, two kayakers off Anacapa Island, a hiker on Mount Larrabee, and a hunting party trapped on the ice near Chefornak, Alaska.
