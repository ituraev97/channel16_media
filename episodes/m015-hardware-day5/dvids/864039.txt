Bull Battery M109A7 Paladin Howitzers take to the field
2022-11-03T00:00:00-04:00
NATO enhanced Forward Presence Battlegroup Poland Soldiers assigned to 2nd Platoon, Bull Battery, 2nd Battalion, 82nd Field Artillery Regiment, 3rd Battalion, 8th Cavalry Regiment, 3rd Armored Brigade Combat Team, 1st Cavalry Division (3-1 ABCT) operationally controlled by the 1st Infantry Division (1 ID), conduct M109A7 Paladin howitzer training during a live fire exercise at Toruń, Poland, Nov. 3, 2022. The 3-1 ABCT is among other units assigned to the 1 ID, proudly working alongside NATO allies and regional security partners to provide combat-credible forces to V Corps, America's forward deployed corps in Europe. (U.S. Army National Guard video by Sgt. Gavin K. Ching)
