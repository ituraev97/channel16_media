USS Chosin Harpoon Launch
2010-07-10T00:00:00-04:00
Pacific Ocean - U.S. Navy Ticonderoga-class guided missile cruiser USS Chosin (CG 65) launches a RGM-84 Harpoon missile during Rim of the Pacific (RIMPAC) 2010 live range training, July 10, 2010. RIMPAC is a large-scale biennial exercise designed to increase mutual cooperation and enhance the tactical capabilities of participating nations in various aspects of maritime operations at sea.
