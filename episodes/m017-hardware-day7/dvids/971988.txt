2nd Distribution Support Battalion conducts live-fire vehicle machine gun range
Date: 2025-07-27
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/971988

U.S. Marines with 2nd Distribution Support Battalion, 2nd Marine Logistics Group, fire a .50 caliber machine gun as part of a vehicle machine gun range on Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune North Carolina July 27 2025. Service members conducted live-fire training with the M2 .50 caliber machine gun mounted on a Joint Light Tactical Vehicle enhancing weapons proficiency platform familiarity and combat readiness.  (U.S. Marine Corps video by Lance Cpl. Javier Santillan)
