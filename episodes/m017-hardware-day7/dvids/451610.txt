2nd LAAD Fires First Stinger Missiles in Norway
Date: 2016-02-23
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/451610

U.S. Marines with Bravo Battery, 2nd Low Altitude Air Defense (LAAD) Battalion fire FIM-92C 
Stinger missiles at Vaagan Range at Orland, Norway, Feb 24, 2016. 2nd LAAD is participating in
Cold Response 16, a Norwegian invitational previously-scheduled exercise that will involve 
approximately 16,000 troops from 12 NATO and partner countries. (U.S. Marine Corps video 
by Sgt. Kathryn K. Bynum/Released)
