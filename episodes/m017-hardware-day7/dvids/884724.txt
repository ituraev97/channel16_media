F-15E Strike Eagle exercise departure
Date: 2023-05-16
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/884724

F-15E Strike Eagles assigned to the 391st Fighter Squadron take off from Kadena Air Base, Japan in support of Exercise Southern Beach, May 16, 2023. Southern Beach is a locally organized bilateral training exercise for U.S. forces and Japan Air Self-Defense units to practice mission planning, flying and debriefing together. (U.S. Air Force photo by Airman 1st Class Jonathan R. Sifuentes)
