F-15E Strike Eagle Capabilities
Date: 2016-11-16
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/493486

The F-15E Strike Eagle serves as the premier fighter jet at Mountain Home Air Force Base and the dual-role fighter has been delivering combat airpower since 1972.  The F-15E was the first U.S. operational aircraft whose engines' thrust exceeded the plane's loaded weight, permitting it to accelerate even while in vertical climb.
