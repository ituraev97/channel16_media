2d LAAD Stinger Live-Fire
Date: 2017-06-01
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/533527

U.S. Marines assigned to 2nd Low Altitude Air Defense Battalion (2D LAAD), participate in an FIM-92 Stinger Missile live-fire range at Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune, N.C., June 1, 2017. 2D LAAD conducted the exercise to familiarize Marines with the Stinger Weapon System as part of an annual training requirement. (U.S. Marine Corps video by Lance Cpl. Zachary M. Ford)
