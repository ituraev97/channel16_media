Stinger Live Fire Exercise
Date: 2025-08-25
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/975005

U.S. Army infantrymen, assigned to the 11th Airborne Division, Fort Wainwright, Alaska, fire the FIM-92 Stinger for the first time ever in Indonesia during exercise Super Garuda Shield 25, in Baturaja, Indonesia, Aug. 26, 2025. Super Garuda Shield is an annual exercise between the Indonesian National Armed Forces and U.S. Military designed to strengthen bilateral interoperability, capabilities and cooperation. (U.S. Army video by Sgt. Nickson Schenk)
