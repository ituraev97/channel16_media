Live-Fire Crew Qualifications, California Army National Guard
Date: 2019-04-12
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/681066

1st Battalion, 185th Infantry Regiment, 81st Brigade of the California Army National Guard was at the Multipurpose Range Complex, Fort Hunter Liggett, California several days in mid-April for live-fire crew qualifications. Eighteen Stryker armored vehicles were on hand, with at least 12 of them mounted with the M2 .50-caliber machine guns. The unit is from the Bakersfield and San Bernardino areas.
