Liberty Wing tests combat capabilities during live missile fire
Date: 2021-03-03
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/786139

U.S. Air Force F-15C Eagles and F-15E Strike Eagles assigned to the 48th Fighter Wing, Royal Air Force Lakenheath, England, fire four AIM-120B missiles during a live fire training mission over the Atlantic Ocean, March 3, 2021.  The training employed munitions against a drone target, testing the pilot’s combat readiness in a peacetime environment to ensure full capability during a wartime setting.
