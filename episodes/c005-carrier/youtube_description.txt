TITLE: 260 Nautical Miles Out: The Coast Guard Rescue That Needed an Aircraft Carrier

November 6, 2025. A sailing catamaran called Magic Bus is filling with water about 260 nautical miles (roughly 300 statute miles) off Cape Hatteras, North Carolina, and the five people aboard have abandoned ship into a life raft. That far out, the rescue pushes the limits of a Coast Guard helicopter's range. Using real U.S. Coast Guard footage and U.S. Navy photos, we follow the call step by step: a Hercules that finds the raft, a Jayhawk crew from Air Station Elizabeth City, and the aircraft carrier USS George H.W. Bush, which refuels the helicopter on the way out and again on the way home. Then, less than two months later, the same air station answers another call off Hatteras: two people, a dog and a cat, and an EPIRB that put their position in front of watchstanders within minutes.
