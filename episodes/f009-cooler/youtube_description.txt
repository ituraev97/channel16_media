TITLE: He Rode Out Hurricane Milton on a Cooler. The Coast Guard Found Him

A Coast Guard rescue from Hurricane Milton: a fishing captain rode out the storm in the open Gulf of Mexico holding on to a cooler, and an Air Station Miami helicopter crew found him about 30 miles off Longboat Key, Florida, on Oct. 10, 2024. Using real U.S. Coast Guard footage, we tell the whole story of two hurricanes in two weeks: Hurricane Helene and the Gulfport rescue, the Capt. Dave's first rescue on Monday, why the captain went back out on landfall day, the lost radio contact, the search, the rescue swimmer, and what the Coast Guard said kept him alive: a life jacket, an EPIRB and a cooler.
