## t1 — FGM-148 Javelin
- "man-portable, fire-and-forget, anti-tank guided missile"; defeats armored vehicles out to 2,500 meters; operationally ready system weighs 49.5 pounds; missile in a disposable launch tube + reusable command launch unit (CLU) with day and night sights; direct-fire or lofted top-attack mode; used by Army and Marine Corps — DOT&E FY2017 Javelin report: https://www.dote.osd.mil/Portals/97/pub/reports/FY2017/army/2017javelin.pdf
- Guides itself after launch so the gunner can take cover; gunner places a cursor on the target, CLU sends lock-on-before-launch; soft launch design → can be fired from inside buildings or bunkers; top-attack climbs above target and strikes where armor is weakest; 50,000th delivery in 2021; Javelin Joint Venture = Lockheed Martin + Raytheon — Lockheed Martin: https://lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/javelin.html
- Soft launch ejects the missile from the tube, then the flight motor ignites; entered service in 1996 with the U.S. Army — CSIS Missile Threat: https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/fgm-148-javelin/
- Footage: 470653 (2nd Cavalry Regiment, Saber Strike 16, Tapa, Estonia, June 19, 2016); 886420 (Weapons Co., 1/23 Marines, Marine Forces Reserve, ITX 4-23, Twentynine Palms, June 9, 2023); 985440 (BLT 1/7, 31st MEU, Camp Fuji, Japan, Oct 9-10, 2025 — only Javelin shots used, no TOW).

## t2 — M58 MICLIC (mine-clearing line charge)
- "rocket-propelled cable that has 1,750 pounds of C4 explosive attached to it"; fired from an Assault Breacher Vehicle; explosion makes live mines detonate or pushes them out of the path; a Marine sergeant: "over 3,600 pounds in the tub that the rocket pulls out" — army.mil (Dec 4, 2013): https://www.army.mil/article/116401/here_comes_the_boom_marines_demonstrate_mine_clearing_line_charge_during_live_fire
- Line is 100 meters long, 1,750 pounds of C4; pulled by a rocket; clears a route 100 m long and about 14-16 m wide depending on terrain; fired from an ABV or an AAV, or towed on a trailer — 2nd Marine Division: https://www.2ndmardiv.marines.mil/News/Article/Article/515857/mine-clearing-line-charges-pave-way-for-ground-troops (only system facts used; the Afghanistan combat use in that article is NOT used)
- "M58 MICLIC is a rocket-projected charge used to clear minefields"; M1150 ABV, 8th BEB, 2nd ABCT, 1st Cavalry Division, near Boleslawiec, Poland, Aug 17, 2023 — DVIDS 894189
- Fort McCoy, Wis.: 469th and 402nd Combat Engineer Companies; "the only approved MICLIC operation in the country this year" — DVIDS 973020 (Aug 3, 2025)
- 4th Combat Engineer Battalion, ITX 4-24, MCAGCC Twentynine Palms, June 11, 2024 (APOBS shots not used) — DVIDS 927608

## t3 — Mk 38 machine gun system
- 25 mm machine gun system for ship self-defense against fast attack craft and fast inshore attack craft; single shot or burst, max 180 rounds/min; effective range 2,500 yards; Mod 2 fielding began 2005 — simple, stabilized, remote-controlled; as of 2018, 341 Mod 2/Mod 3 delivered; installed on CG, CVN, DDG, LSD, LHD, LHA, LCC, PC… and USCG FRC — navy.mil fact file: https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2167836/mk-38-25-mm-machine-gun-system/
- Mk 38 Mod 4: 30 mm (Mk 44 Bushmaster II), "first caliber increase in more than 30 years"; close-in defense especially against unmanned aerial systems and fast unmanned surface vehicles; first ship USS Mustin (DDG 89) — NAVSEA/NSWCDD (Feb 6, 2026): https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/News/Article-View/Article/4398950/nswcdd-delivers-next-generation-mk-38-defense-system/

## t5 — SM-3 Block IIA (Aegis BMD)
- FTM-44, Nov 16, 2020 (Hawaii time): MDA + USS John Finn intercepted and destroyed a threat-representative ICBM target with an SM-3 Block IIA, northeast of Hawaii; target launched from Reagan Test Site, Kwajalein Atoll; ship got tracking data via C2BMC and fired on remote engagement; Block IIA originally designed for intermediate-range ballistic missiles; Aegis BMD jointly managed by MDA and Navy — INDOPACOM: https://www.pacom.mil/Media/News/News-Articles/Article/2418836/us-successfully-conducts-sm-3-block-iia-intercept-test-against-an-intercontinen/ ; DVIDS 772961/772985
- SM-3 Block IIA: joint U.S.-Japan cooperative program; "21-inch diameter variant" — MDA: https://www.mda.mil/system/aegis_bmd.html
- Hit-to-kill: kinetic force rather than an explosive warhead; impact comparable to a 10-ton truck at 600 mph; more than 30 successful space intercepts; more than 400 interceptors delivered to U.S. and Japanese navies — RTX/Raytheon: https://www.rtx.com/raytheon/what-we-do/strategic-missile-defense/sm-3-interceptor
- SFTM-01, Feb 3, 2017: MDA, Japan MoD and USS John Paul Jones — first intercept of a ballistic missile target using SM-3 Block IIA, off the west coast of Hawaii; target = medium-range ballistic missile launched from Pacific Missile Range Facility, Kauai (DVIDS 508345 + its title cards)
