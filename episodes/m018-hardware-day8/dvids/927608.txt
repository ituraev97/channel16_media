4th Combat Engineer Battalion shoots explosive line charges at ITX 4-24
Date: 2024-06-11
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/927608

U.S. Marines with 4th Combat Engineer Battalion, 4th Marine Division, Marine Forces Reserve, conduct fire an anti-personnel obstacle breaching system and mine clearing line charge on range 114 during Integrated Training Exercise 4-24 at Marine Corps Air-Ground Combat Center Twentynine Palms, California, June 11, 2024. As the Marine Corps Reserve’s premier annual training event, ITX provides opportunities to mobilize geographically dispersed forces for a deployment; increase combat readiness and lethality; and exercise MAGTF command and control of battalions and squadrons across the full spectrum of warfare. (U.S. Marine Corps photo by Sgt. Ethan M. LeBlanc)
