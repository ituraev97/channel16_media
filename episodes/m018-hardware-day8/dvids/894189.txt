MICLIC B-Roll
Date: 2023-08-17
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/894189

An M1150 Assault Breacher Vehicle assigned to 8th Brigade Engineer Battalion, 2nd Armored Brigade Combat Team, 1st Cavalry Division, fires an M58 Mine Clearing Line Charge (MICLIC) in a training area near Boleslawiec, Poland, Aug. 17, 2023. The M58 MICLIC is a rocket-projected charge used to clear minefields.
