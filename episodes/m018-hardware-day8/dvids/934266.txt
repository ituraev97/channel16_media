Wasp conducts live fire exercise
Date: 2024-08-05
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/934266

MEDITERRANEAN SEA (Aug. 2, 2024) An MK-38 25mm machine gun system fires during a live-fire exercise as part of a sustainment shoot aboard the amphibious assault ship USS Wasp (LHD 1), Aug. 2, 2024. Wasp is conducting operations in the U.S. Naval Forces Europe and Africa (NAVEUR-NAVAF) area of operations as the flagship of the Wasp Amphibious Ready Group (WSP ARG)-24th MEU (SOC). The WSP ARG-24th MEU (SOC) is on a scheduled deployment to the NAVEUR-NAVAF area of operations, supporting U.S., Allied and partner interests in the region, including in the Eastern Mediterranean Sea, to continue promoting regional stability and deterring aggression. (U.S. Navy video by Mass Communication Specialist 2nd Class Sydney Milligan)
