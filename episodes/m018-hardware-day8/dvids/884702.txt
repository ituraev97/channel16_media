B-Roll: HMLA-367 Hellfire Live Fire
Date: 2023-05-23
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/884702

U.S. Marines with Marine Light Attack Helicopter Squadron (HMLA) 367, Marine Aircraft Group (MAG) 39, 3rd Marine Aircraft Wing (MAW), conduct AGM-114 Hellfire live fire training on San Clemente Island, California, May 23, 2023. MAG-39 conducted a series of training evolutions to validate and further develop expeditionary intelligence, command and control, maritime reconnaissance/strike, and electronic warfare tactics, techniques, and procedures. (U.S. Marine Corps video by Sgt. Joshua Brittenham.)
