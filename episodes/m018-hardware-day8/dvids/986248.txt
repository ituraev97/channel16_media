Dogface engineers clear minefields
Date: 2025-10-19
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/986248

U.S. Army 2nd Lt. Luke Sperra, assigned to 9th Engineer Battalion, 2nd Armored Brigade Combat Team, 3rd Infantry Division, discusses the process of the MICLIC Training, which includes the proper way to execute an in-stride breach and how to properly discharge the MICLIC safely to ensure combat readiness for Soldiers at Fort Stewart, Georgia, Oct. 19, 2025. The M58 MICLIC is a rocket-projected charge used to clear minefields. (U.S. Army video by Spc. Darnell Howard)
