# STORM FILES 006 — Hurricane Florence (North Carolina, September 2018): sources

Every fact below comes from a page opened during research (Nov 4, 2026). Rescued people are private: no names are used, even where news reports name them.

## The storm
- **NWS Wilmington, "Hurricane Florence"** — https://www.weather.gov/ilm/HurricaneFlorence
  - Large, slow-moving Category 1; made landfall the morning of Sept 14, 2018; eye crossed Wrightsville Beach at 7:15 a.m.; max sustained winds near 90 mph.
  - Aug 31 tropical depression; Sept 1 tropical storm; Sept 5 peak 130 mph; Sept 10 strengthens to 140 mph; Sept 14 landfall.
  - Highest total 35.93" 6.2 mi NW of Elizabethtown, NC; Loris, SC 23.63" = new SC tropical-cyclone record; Wilmington airport 23.02".
  - Lumber River at Lumberton crest est. near 25.4 ft, "over one foot higher than the Hurricane Matthew crest in 2016".
  - Northeast Cape Fear at Burgaw 25.58 ft early Sept 19, exceeding the Floyd crest by over three feet.
  - Cape Fear River crested between two and six feet higher than during Matthew; record at Lock and Dam #1.
  - Fatalities (state totals): North Carolina 42, South Carolina 9.
- **NHC Intermediate Advisory 60A, 8:00 AM EDT Fri Sept 14, 2018** — https://nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2018/al06/al062018.public_a.060.shtml
  - About 10 mi S of Wilmington; max sustained 90 mph; movement W (260°) at 6 mph; headline "CATASTROPHIC FRESHWATER FLOODING EXPECTED OVER PORTIONS OF NORTH AND SOUTH CAROLINA"; additional 20–25 in., isolated storm totals 30–40 in.
- **NOAA Weather Prediction Center, Florence rainfall page** — https://www.wpc.ncep.noaa.gov/tropical/rain/florence2018.html
  - Tropical wave left the African coast as a low near Senegal Aug 30; slowed its forward motion after landfall; "Due to its slow movement, Florence became the wettest known tropical cyclone on record for the Carolinas."
- **NASA GPM** — https://gpm.nasa.gov/applications/weather/hurricane-florence-makes-landfall-brings-torrential-rains-and-record-flooding-carolinas
  - Peak Category 4, 140 mph; eyewall replacement Sept 11 reduced peak intensity while increasing size; "slowed down as it approached the coast"; landfall ~7:15 a.m. EDT near Wrightsville Beach at 90 mph; over 35 in. in Elizabethtown, 33 in. in Swansboro.
- **weather.com recap** — https://weather.com/storms/hurricane/news/2018-09-19-hurricane-florence-carolinas-flooding-wind-storm-surge-recap
  - 10.1-ft storm surge recorded very early Sept 14 in New Bern; NC rainfall 35.93" near Elizabethtown vs. previous record 24.06" (Floyd, 1999); Loris 23.63" vs. 17.45" (Beryl 1994); Wilmington wettest year on record, with over three months left in 2018.
- **WECT, "Remembering Florence"** — https://www.wect.com/2019/09/16/remembering-florence-vast-storm-that-dwarfed-its-low-category
  - Elizabethtown about 50 miles northwest of landfall; Florence stayed over the Carolinas for four days.

## The Coast Guard response (official)
- **The Long Blue Line: Coast Guard's storm warriors fight Hurricane Florence** (U.S. Coast Guard History, Lt. Cmdr. Ace Castle) — https://www.history.uscg.mil/Research/THE-LONG-BLUE-LINE/Article/2929404/the-long-blue-line-coast-guards-storm-warriors-fight-hurricane-florence/
  - "this hurricane was a flooding event, and flooding events require boats" (author's text; helicopters vital, boats essential).
  - Rocky Point: Lt. Weston Dodson's MH-60 crew responded to a family in distress, landed in a grassy field; rescue swimmers Chris Fisher and John Fuller went to locate the family; two local teachers volunteered to help reach stranded residents; multiple trips; the teachers were the last two of 37 community members rescued by that helicopter (they had refused to leave; the swimmers persuaded them). Caption: Air Station Elizabeth City MH-60T evacuated 26 adults, 11 children, seven dogs, four cats.
  - Totals: rescued or assisted over 900 victims and 300 pets in NC and SC; over 3,000 Coast Guard members, multiple cutters, 35 shallow-water rescue boats, 28 aircraft.
- **USCG news release, Sept 15, 2018 (GovDelivery)** — https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/20d9577
  - Aircrews on seven helicopters (four HH-65 Dolphins, three MH-60 Jayhawks) rescued 57 people and eight pets across NC on Sat. Sept 15; videos 625750 and 625791.
- **USCG news release, Sept 18, 2018 (GovDelivery)** — https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/20e2a2f
  - 426 people and 234 pets rescued since Florence began; 26 shallow-water response boat teams with 116 people deployed to NC; 191 members at the NC Incident Command Post in Goldsboro; Capt. Bion Stewart quote.
- **WorkBoat, Sept 18, 2018** — https://www.workboat.com/coastal-inland-waterways/hundreds-of-small-boat-rescues-after-hurricane-florence
  - (context) 434 people and 234 pets by midday Tuesday; 35 shallow-water rescue boat teams across the region; Elizabethtown nearly 36 in. in four days.

## Safety
- **NWS, "Turn Around Don't Drown"** — https://weather.gov/tsa/hydro_tadd
  - "A mere six inches of fast-moving flood water can knock over an adult." "A foot of water will float many vehicles." "It takes only two feet of rushing water to carry away most vehicles." Citing the CDC: "over half of all flood-related drownings occur when a vehicle is driven into hazardous flood water".
