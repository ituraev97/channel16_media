U.S. Marines with Logistics Operations School conduct a helicopter support team exercise at Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune, North Carolina, Feb. 22, 2024. The exercise is designed to prepare Marines to manage activities at landing zones: facilitate the pickup, movement and landing of helicopter-borne troops, equipment, and supplies.
