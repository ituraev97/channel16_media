## I — The job (AST rating)
- gocoastguard.com AST — https://gocoastguard.com/careers/enlisted/ast — rescue swimmers who save lives, provide emergency medical support, maintain survival equipment; fly on helicopters into sea rescues, land flooding, cliff rescues; 22-week course = 7-wk EMT (NREMT), 10-wk rescue swimmer, 5-wk aviation life support; usually assigned to air stations; "AST tasks require the highest degree of physical fitness."
- USCG release, Aug 31, 2022 (TRACEN Petaluma first class) — https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/3145622/ — pool at ATTC Elizabeth City closed by a facilities problem; 75% finished rescue swimmer training, historically high; Capt. Matthew Chong: "The Coast Guard has a critical shortage of aviation survival technicians throughout the enterprise"

## VI — Advanced Helicopter Rescue School
- MyCG, May 14, 2026 "Forging responders in the Graveyard of the Pacific" — https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/4485729/forging-responders-in-the-graveyard-of-the-pacific/ — Astoria, mouth of the Columbia, "Graveyard of the Pacific"; trainees describe consistent 10–15-ft waves as among the biggest swells in the country; cliffs (rappelling from a hovering helicopter), caves (currents and seas building); ~16 students per cadre: 8 swimmers, 4 flight mechanics, 4 pilots; swimmers attend within 3 years of AST A-School and return at regular intervals; founded by rescue swimmers ~30 years ago; LCDR Danielle Benedetto quote
- DVIDS 1006811 (AHRS, Feb 2026), 987403 (AHRS ASTs + Air Force PJs, Fort Stevens State Park, Nov 19, 2025; black helicopters are Air Force)
