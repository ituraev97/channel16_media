TITLE: What a Coast Guard Rescue Swimmer Actually Does, in 5 Real Rescues

What does a Coast Guard rescue swimmer actually do? Five real U.S. Coast Guard rescues, in real Coast Guard footage, show the job of an aviation survival technician (AST) from the jump to the hoist. A breaking wave flips a boat off the Columbia River bar while the swimmer is already in the water; three people in life jackets on Lake Michigan; a medical emergency near Nahma; a swimmer who stays behind off Sargent, Texas; a pump lowered on the hoist cable near Galveston; and the Advanced Helicopter Rescue School in Astoria, where rescue swimmers train in 10- to 15-foot surf.
