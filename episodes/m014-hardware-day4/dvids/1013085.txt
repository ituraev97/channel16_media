Soldiers with the 370th Armored Combat Engineer Company (CEC-A) conducted a rigorous Bradley Crew Gunnery training exercise at the Grafenwöhr Training Area in Germany May 13th 2026. The 370th CEC‑A, U.S. Army Europe and Africa’s only forward‑stationed armored engineer formation, sharpened its lethality and reinforced mission readiness through sustained, high‑intensity training.
