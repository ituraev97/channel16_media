Three U.S. Air Force B-2 Spirit stealth bombers and supporting forces from the 131st Bomb Wing, 509th Bomb Wing and Guam deployed for a Bomber Task Force mission at Royal Australian Air Force Base Amberley, Australia, Aug. 8–Sept. 17, 2024.
