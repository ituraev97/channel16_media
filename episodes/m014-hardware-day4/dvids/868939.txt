The 509th Logistics Readiness Squadron (LRS) petroleum, oil and lubricants (POL) flight travels with the B-2 Spirit bomber to train on Agile Combat Employment at Edwards Air Force Base, California, December 2, 2022. The POL team worked with half the manning but same amount of work, still finishing the mission in the same amount of time. (U.S. Air Force B-Roll by Airman 1st Class Joseph Garcia)
