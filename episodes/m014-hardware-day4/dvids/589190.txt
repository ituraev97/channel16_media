A graphic display of USS Hartford (SSN 768) surfacing through ice during Ice Exercise 2018 (ICEX). ICEX 2018 is a five-week exercise that allows the U.S. Navy to assess its operational readiness in the Arctic, increase experience in the region, advance understanding of the Arctic environment, and continue to develop relationships with other services, allies and partner organizations.
