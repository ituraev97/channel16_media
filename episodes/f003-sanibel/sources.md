## Storm
- Landfall 3:05 p.m. EDT Sept. 28, 2022 near Cayo Costa, Lee County; Cat 4, 150 mph; 940 mb (Air Force Reserve Hurricane Hunters); eyewall reached Sanibel and Captiva shortly after noon; NHC forecast surge up to 18 ft in Lee and Charlotte counties; surge above 7 ft in Naples before landfall; high tide 7:06 p.m.; Jamie Rhome "the time for evacuation has long passed." https://www.opb.org/article/2022/09/28/hurricane-ian-update-landfall-southwest-florida-category-4-storm/ (NPR)
- NOAA final report (April 2023): Cat 5, ~161 mph on Sept. 28 after passing the Dry Tortugas; Cat 4 150 mph at landfall; surge 10–15 ft above ground level at Fort Myers Beach, up to 13 ft on Sanibel; >$112B U.S. damage; costliest hurricane in Florida history, third-costliest in U.S.; Florida direct deaths 66, including 41 storm-surge drownings. https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/noaa-ian-was-cat-5-before-weakening-at-florida-landfall (AP)
- Storm surge definition ("abnormal rise of water generated by a storm, over and above the predicted astronomical tides"; water pushed toward shore by the winds). https://www2.whoi.edu/site/coastalgroup/about/what-we-study/primers/storm-surge
- Second landfall near Georgetown, S.C., Cat 1, 85 mph; >200,000 without power in S.C.; Coast Guard saved 150+ people and 50+ pets (Adm. Linda Fagan, Friday); Capt. Robert Kinsey, District Seven: "taking to the sea during a hurricane is flat out reckless." https://maritime-executive.com/article/u-s-coast-guard-completes-150-plus-rescues-after-hurricane-ian

## Sanibel
- Causeway: at least five sections destroyed (Lee County officials); only road access; >3 million vehicles/yr (2017 city count); Sanibel ~6,400 residents (Census, April 2021); roughly 200 households did not evacuate; 12 rescued with injuries and ~40 without; dozens still stranded Thursday night; Mayor Holly Smith: "Frankly, no."; DeSantis "biblical storm surge", rebuild "not something that will happen overnight"; Lee County Manager Roger Desjarlais quote; 15 shelters, ~4,000 people by late Wednesday. https://kvia.com/cnn-national/2022/09/29/sanibel-and-captiva-islands-cut-off-from-florida-mainland-after-ians-storm-surge-washes-away-three-parts-of-sanibel-causeway-2/ (CNN)

## Coast Guard response
- Saturday Oct. 1: 442 people and 89 pets saved or assisted in Florida and South Carolina; more than 118 flights since Wednesday; Coast Guard videos show crews from Air Stations Clearwater, Miami (and St. Petersburg, per Fox — not used). https://www.foxnews.com/us/hurricane-ian-coast-guard-rescues-two-people-dog-stranded-sanibel-island
- Flight mechanic (Savannah Brewer): survivors signaled with flashlights, messages in the sand, waving; crews flew day and night for people on the barrier islands who wanted to leave; 200+ people and 50 animals in the first days. https://www.tampabay28.com/weather/hurricane-ian/clearwater-coast-guard-rescues-over-200-residents-in-first-two-days-after-hurricane-ian-hits-florida
- Oct. 6: 746 people and 94 pets rescued; reunion of Clearwater Jayhawk crew with 67-year-old boater (44-ft boat, moving it out of Ian's path, anchored near landfall, phone died, feared hypothermia from cold rain, EPIRB, crew arrived ~2:30 a.m. Thursday by his account, swimmer lowered by basket onto boat, near Pine Island; quotes "That was the sweetest sound I ever heard." and "Don't panic. That's the big thing. You've got to keep a level head."; plaque with a boat propeller). Name not used. https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2022/10/06/coast-guard-hurricane-ian-
- How a 406 MHz beacon alert reaches rescuers (satellites → local user terminal computes location → Mission Control Center → Rescue Coordination Center; "almost instantaneously"; quicker when registered). https://www.sarsat.noaa.gov/sys-diag.html
- MH-60T: medium range, crew 4, 700 nm. https://www.dcms.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/Assistant-Commandant-for-Acquisitions-CG-9/Programs/Air-Programs/MRR-MH-60T/MRR-Program-Profile/ ; MH-65: short range, 350 nm, 148 kt cruise, 3 h endurance. https://dcms.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/Assistant-Commandant-for-Acquisitions-CG-9/Programs/Air-Programs/SRR-MH-65/SRR-Program-Profile
- AST training 22 weeks (EMT, rescue swimmer, aviation life support/survival equipment). https://gocoastguard.com/careers/enlisted/ast
