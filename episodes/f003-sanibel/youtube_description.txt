TITLE: Hurricane Ian Cut Sanibel Island Off. The Coast Guard Became the Bridge.

September 2022. Hurricane Ian comes ashore in southwest Florida as a Category 4, and its storm surge tears apart the Sanibel Causeway, the island's only road to the mainland. Using real U.S. Coast Guard helmet-camera and hoist footage, we follow the crews from Air Station Miami and Air Station Clearwater as they lift people, dogs and cats off a cut-off island: a flooded street, a boat stranded deep in the mangroves, a rooftop, and a boater with a dead phone and a distress beacon.
