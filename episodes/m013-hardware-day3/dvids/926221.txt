U.S. Marines fire M777 Howitzers during Artillery Relocation Training Program 24.1 at Combined Arms Training Center Camp Fuji, Japan, May 16, 2024. The training at ARTP enhances the expertise and preparedness of the Marine Corps’ sole permanently forward-deployed artillery unit, increasing their ability to deliver precise indirect fires support. The Marines are with 3d Battalion, 12th Marine Littoral Regiment, 3d Marine Division. (U.S. Marine Corps video by Lance Cpl. Cara Castaneda)
