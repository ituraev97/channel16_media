## t4 — Tomahawk
Page: U.S. Navy fact file, Tomahawk Cruise Missile — https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169229/tomahawk-cruise-missile/
- Range "900 nautical miles (1000 statute miles, 1600 km)"; "Williams International 415-400 turbofan engine; ARC/CSD solid-fuel booster"; flies at "high subsonic speeds" at "extremely low altitudes"; launched from "surface ships and U.S. Navy and United Kingdom Royal Navy submarines"; Block II IOC 1984; Block IV can be retargeted in flight to one of 15 preset targets or any GPS coordinate, can loiter and send battle damage imagery.
Page: NAVAIR, Tomahawk — https://www.navair.navy.mil/product/Tomahawk
- Length "20.3 feet"; weight "3,330 lbs. with rocket motor"; wingspan "8 feet 6 inches"; Block V unit cost "Approximately $3.64M (FY26 $)".
Page: Raytheon, "U.S. Navy, Raytheon demonstrate network-enabled Tomahawk cruise missiles in flight" (Oct 5, 2015) — https://raytheon.mediaroom.com/2015-10-05-US-Navy,-Raytheon-demonstrate-network-enabled-Tomahawk-cruise-missiles-in-flight
- USS Gridley (DDG 101) test off San Nicolas Island; missile retargeted mid-flight from U.S. Fifth Fleet headquarters in Bahrain; sent a reconnaissance photo over UHF SATCOM; then loitered.
Page: Raytheon, moving-target capability (Feb 11, 2015) — https://raytheon.mediaroom.com/2015-02-11-US-Navy-Raytheon-demonstrate-moving-target-capability-for-Tomahawk-Block-IV ; NAVAIR — https://www.navair.navy.mil/node/21916
- USS Kidd (DDG 100), Jan 2015: second test was a Marine call-for-fire on San Nicolas Island, ending in a vertical-dive "direct hit".
DVIDS 424260 (slate: "USS Gridley (DDG 101) Flight Test 464 Tomahawk Launch, 1 September 2015"). DVIDS 410456: TLAM Flight Test 409, I MEF Marines, San Nicolas Island, Jan 28, 2015.

## t5 — Laser Weapon System (LaWS), USS Ponce
Page: ONR, "Historic Leap: Navy Shipboard Laser Operates in Persian Gulf" — https://www.onr.navy.mil/media-center/news-releases/historic-leap-navy-shipboard-laser-operates-persian-gulf

Page: ONR, "All Systems Go: Navy's Laser Weapon Ready for Summer Deployment" — https://www.onr.navy.mil/media-center/news-releases/all-systems-go-navys-laser-weapon-ready-summer-deployment

Page: Live Science on the July 2017 CNN demo — https://www.livescience.com/59846-navy-laser-weapon-blasts-drones.html
