1st Air Cavalry Brigade conducts AH-64 Apache Table IV training B-roll
Date: 2026-09-10
URL: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1023301

Troopers assigned to 1st Battalion, 227th Aviation Regiment, piloting AH‑64 Apaches, conduct Table IV aerial gunnery training at Forward Area Refueling Point East on Fort Hood, Texas, Sept. 10, 2026. Table IV training evaluates pilots on their ability to employ the AH-64’s weapons systems and meet required gunnery standards. (U.S. Army video by Sgt. 1st Class Kevin A. D. Spence and Staff Sgt. Zelika P. Stewart)
