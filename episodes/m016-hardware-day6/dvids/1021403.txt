U.S. Air Force Maj. Taylor "FEMA" Hiester, F-16 Viper Demonstration Team commander and pilot, performs aerial maneuvers in an F-16C Fighting Falcon during Oshkosh Airventure 2026, Oshkosh, Wisconsin, July 29, 2026. The team performs at over 20 airshows annually across the U.S. and abroad demonstrating the capabilities of the F-16 and the professionalism of the Airmen who operate and maintain them.
