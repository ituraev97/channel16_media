TITLE: 31 Aboard a Burning, Sinking Ship: The Coast Guard and Canada's Overnight Rescue

On the night of March 2, 2021, the Canadian scallop trawler Atlantic Destiny had a catastrophic engine failure about 120 nautical miles south of Yarmouth, Nova Scotia: an engine that would not stop, a fire, and seawater pouring into the hull, with 31 people aboard, winds gusting to 55 knots and water near 5 °C. Using real U.S. Coast Guard footage and the Transportation Safety Board of Canada's investigation, we follow the night step by step: the relayed distress call, the Royal Canadian Air Force Hercules and Cormorant, two Air Station Cape Cod Jayhawks that hoisted 21 fishermen, the last six taken off by the Canadian Coast Guard ship Cape Roger, and what the TSB found about why the ship was lost.
