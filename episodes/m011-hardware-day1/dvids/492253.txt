Barry M. Goldwater Range 75th Anniversary | 2016-11-10T00:00:00-05:00 
 The 75th anniversary of the Barry M. Goldwater Range was celebrated at the Papago Park Military Reservation in Phoenix, Ariz., Nov. 10. Gov. Doug Ducey was the honored guest and every branch of military service was represented at the celebration.
