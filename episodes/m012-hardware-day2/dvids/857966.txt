USS Shoup Launches SM-2 Missile During Live Fire With a Purpose Exercise | 2022-09-18T00:00:00-04:00 
 The Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer USS Shoup (DDG 86) launches an SM-2 Block IIIA missile in the Pacific Ocean as part of a Live Fire With a Purpose exercise Sept. 18, 2022. A data analytics team from Naval Surface Warfare Center, Corona Division, embarked on Shoup to provide data recording and analysis of the launch. (U.S. Navy video by Neil Mabini)
