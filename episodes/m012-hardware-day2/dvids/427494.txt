Combined Resolve V: Abrams M1A2 Gunnery at Grafenwoehr | 2015-10-08T00:00:00-04:00 
 U.S. soldiers, assigned to 1st Battalion, 64th Armor Regiment, 1st Brigade Combat Team, 3rd Infantry Division, fire M1A2 Sepv2 Abrams Main Battle Tanks during Exercise Combined Resolve V at the 7th Army Joint Multinational Training Command's Grafenwoehr Training Area, Germany, Oct. 08, 2015. Combined Resolve V is designed to exercise the U.S. Army’s regionally aligned force to the U.S. European Command area of responsibility with multinational training at all echelons.
