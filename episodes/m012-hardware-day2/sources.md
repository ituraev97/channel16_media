## Official fact sheets / articles
- [NAVY-MK45] U.S. Navy fact file, MK 45 5-inch 54/62 caliber guns — https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2167864/mk-45-5-inch-5462-caliber-guns/
  - fully-automatic naval gun mount; against ships, aircraft and shore targets; rate of fire 16–20 rounds/min automatic, conventional ammunition
  - range 13 nautical miles (14.9 statute miles) with conventional ammunition; magazine 600 rounds (destroyers), 1,200 rounds (cruisers)
  - full crew (Mount Captain, EP-2 Operator and four ammunition handlers) operates from below deck
  - developed as lighter weight, more easily maintained replacement for the MK 42; MOD 4 adds a longer 62-caliber barrel to improve shore bombardment; deployed 1971 (Mod 0); on DDG 51 and CG 47 class ships
- [NAVY-SM] U.S. Navy fact file, Standard Missile — https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169011/standard-missile/
  - SM-2: medium-long range shipboard surface-to-air missile; "the U.S Navy's primary surface-to-air air defense weapon"; AEGIS Weapon System; Ticonderoga-class cruisers and Arleigh Burke-class destroyers; Mark 41 vertical launcher
  - SM-2 Block III/IIIA/IIIB (medium range): length 15 ft 6 in; weight 1,558 lb; range up to 90 nautical miles; dual thrust solid fuel rocket; semi-active radar homing; deployed 1981 (SM-2 MR)
- [NAVY-FORD-1ST] Navy.mil, "USS Gerald R. Ford Completes First Arrested Landing and Launch" — https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/Press-Releases/display-pressreleases/Article/2252477/uss-gerald-r-ford-completes-first-arrested-landing-and-launch/
  - first arrested landing and catapult launch July 28, 2017 (F/A-18F, VX-23); EMALS "mission and function … remains the same as the traditional steam catapult" but entirely different technology; smoother acceleration, more accurate end-speed control
  - AAG: modular, integrated, software-controlled; energy absorbers, power conditioning equipment and digital controls
